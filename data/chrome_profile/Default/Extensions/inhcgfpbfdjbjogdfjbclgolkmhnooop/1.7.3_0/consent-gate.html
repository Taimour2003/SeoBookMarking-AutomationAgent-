Welcome to AI Sidebar with Deepseek, ChatGPT, Claude and more.

This extension lets you chat with AI from a sidebar on any website. Using it involves some processing of your data that is necessary to make the AI work — here is exactly what happens, so you know before you start.

Necessary to provide the service:

  • AI chat — the messages you type are sent to the AI provider to generate a reply. Without this the sidebar cannot function.
  • aitopia.ai — the technology provider that powers the sidebar (acting as our processor).
  • Your preferences — stored locally in your browser only.
ℹ️ Privacy Notice: The messages you send are processed by the AI provider to generate responses. We do not sell your data and the extension does not collect analytics or track the pages you visit.

Prefer not to use it? Just close this tab — the extension stays inactive until you continue — or remove it from your browser at any time.
